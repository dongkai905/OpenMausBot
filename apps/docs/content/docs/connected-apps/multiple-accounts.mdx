MausBot supports several labeled accounts for the same mail service. This is useful for a work inbox and a personal one, or two mailboxes at the same provider.

## Add an account

1. Open **Connected apps**.
2. Find the Email app.
3. Choose **Connect** (or **Add account** if you already have one).
4. Enter the address, and a unique label such as `work` or `personal`.
5. Complete the sign-in with your mail provider's password or app password.

Each mailbox appears separately with its label and status. A new account never silently becomes the default for the old one: a bot names the account it is working with, and the person sees which one answered.

## Disconnect safely

Disconnect removes only the chosen mailbox and leaves the others active. The credentials of a removed mailbox are deleted with it.

## Grants apply to tools, not accounts

Per-bot tool grants say which mail tools a bot may call; they do not pick a mailbox. A grant covers every mailbox of that service on the installation, so to keep one bot away from a specific mailbox, use a separate MausBot installation or configuration.
